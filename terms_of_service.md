## 1. What the Bot does

The Bot performs automated background checks on recruitment applications. It reads messages in a designated channel, resolves the applicant's identity across Discord and Roblox, checks that identity against external ban databases, watchlists, and blacklists, and then either grants a Discord role and sends an acceptance DM, or replies with a generic reason for failing.

The Bot contacts third-party services to perform these checks. A list of these services is in the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).
